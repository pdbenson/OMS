🪙 Mint TPT

Mint new TPT when you have deposited real USD into the TAPAT bank account. This increases total USD in the system and mints TPT proportionally.

⚠️ Bank Deposit Confirmation Required
Before minting, confirm: Have you actually deposited real USD into the TAPAT bank account?

Minting TPT without a real bank deposit creates unbacked tokens and breaks the ledger.
